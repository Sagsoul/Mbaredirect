02 — Logo

Logo system

The wordmark is the primary identity. Use the full lockup on most surfaces. The mark alone is used only as an app icon or favicon.

Mbare
Direct
Mbare

Clear space

Always maintain a minimum clear space of 1× the cap-height of the wordmark on all sides. Never crowd the logo against other elements.

Logo misuse

Never stretch, rotate, recolour, or add effects to the logo. Never place it on a background without sufficient contrast.

✓ Do
Use on approved backgrounds
Green on white, white on dark, white on brand green — all approved combinations.
✕ Don't
Place on busy imagery
Never overlay the logo on a photograph or complex pattern without a sufficient scrim.
✓ Do
Scale proportionally
Lock aspect ratio when resizing. Minimum print size: 25mm. Minimum digital: 120px wide.
✕ Don't
Retype or recreate the logo
Never set the logo manually in any typeface. Always use the approved SVG or PNG assets.

06 — Voice & Tone

How we speak

Mbare Direct speaks like a knowledgeable friend at the market — direct, warm, and respectful of the buyer's time.

✓ We say

"Post what you need. Verified sellers pitch their best price."
"Your order is confirmed. The seller will contact you via WhatsApp."
"3 sellers are competing for your request right now."

✕ We don't say

"Leverage our cutting-edge AI-powered marketplace synergy platform."
"An error has occurred. Please contact support with error code 4291."
"Kindly be advised that your transaction is in a pending state."
✓ Tone
Short sentences, active voice
Write as you would speak at a market stall. Buyers are busy.
✕ Tone
Jargon and corporate-speak
Avoid words like "leverage", "synergy", "ecosystem", or "seamless experience".
