Our Brand

Four traits that define us

Every design decision — colour, type, copy, layout — should reinforce at least one of these traits.

01

Trustworthy

Buyers come to us because we protect them first. Verified sellers, transparent pricing.

02

Grounded

We speak plainly. No jargon, no hype — just clear, honest language rooted in Zimbabwe.

03

Energetic

Markets are alive. Our voice has pace; our layouts breathe; nothing feels static.

04

Warm

The amber accent carries the heat of Mbare market. We are human, not corporate.

Logo System

The mark & wordmark

The brand mark is an M drawn as a single continuous stroke — a nod to the market's energy and the movement of goods. Use the full lockup whenever space allows.

MbareDirect

MbareDirect
On dark — reversed lockup

Logo usage rules

✓ Do
  • Always use the approved SVG source files
  • Maintain the minimum clear space (= height of the "M" stroke)
  • Use on the approved brand colours only
  • Scale proportionally from the centre
✗ Don't
  • Don't recolour the mark outside brand colours
  • Don't add drop shadows or effects to the mark
  • Don't stretch, rotate, or distort the logo
  • Don't place the logo on busy backgrounds

Voice & Tone

How we speak

Our voice is consistent. Our tone adapts to context — celebratory in wins, plain and calm in errors, urgent in time-sensitive moments.

Buyers

Empowering language. You are in control — sellers compete for you.

"Post your need in 60 seconds. Let sellers pitch to you."

Sellers

Respect and partnership. We help you reach ready buyers.

"Verified sellers unlock 10× more pitch opportunities."

Errors

Plain and helpful. No blame, always an action step.

"We couldn't send your pitch — check your connection and try again."

Success states

Warm and specific. Tell the user exactly what happened.

"Pitch sent! Taurai will see it within the hour."

UI Components

Buttons & form inputs

Components follow an 8px grid and use 8px border-radius. Touch targets are a minimum 44px tall on mobile.
